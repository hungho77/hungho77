About

I’m Hung Thinh Ho, a Senior Edge AI Engineer working at the intersection of robot learning and efficient inference. I build and optimize vision-language-action (VLA) systems, with a focus on making capable models practical on resource-constrained hardware.

My work spans low-bit quantization, C++ inference runtimes, and model deployment on edge GPUs. My research also explores world models and retrieval for robust robotic execution.
